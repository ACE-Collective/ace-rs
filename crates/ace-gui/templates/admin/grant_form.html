Grant a permission

A grant gives what its pattern covers, listed beside each grant above. A deny wins over any allow. A grant covering user:write lets its holder change any grant, their own included, so they can give themselves *:*. Give it only to someone you'd make an administrator.

A pattern is major:minor, and either half may hold *, ? or [...].
